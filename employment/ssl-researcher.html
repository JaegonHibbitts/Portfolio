UMD Undergraduate Researcher

Space Systems Laboratory · Dec 2024 – Present

Description

Worked on improvements to ASTRA, an Arduino-controlled over-terrain vehicle, with emphasis on computer control and future autonomy. My work used ROS 2 and Python on a Raspberry Pi to communicate with pre-existing Arduino C++ controls over serial.

Later responsibilities expanded to coordinating ongoing ASTRA project work, developing heartbeat-based communication safety, and pursuing sensor-informed autonomy and SLAM-oriented navigation.
