MESA Mobile App Project

Team Lead · Mobile Application Design

Description

As team lead, I helped design and develop a mobile application for the Maryland Emergency Shelter Association. The app concept supported volunteer coordination during emergency shelter operations, including check-in, check-out, and resource allocation workflows.

My role combined interface design, feature planning, and development leadership while working with the team to align the application with stakeholder needs.
